01 · Background

Living next to each other, but not connected

University neighborhoods are full of international students and Korean residents who could easily help each other, but there's no simple, bilingual place for them to meet and exchange.

The problem

  • Most local marketplace apps are built mainly for Korean speakers, which is hard for newcomers.
  • Foreigners struggle with everyday tasks: moving, repairs, translating documents.
  • Students leave furniture and appliances behind every semester, and good items get thrown away.
  • Finding a reliable language exchange partner nearby depends on luck.
  • It's hard to know whether a stranger online can be trusted.

Neighborly's solution

  • A bilingual (English / 한국어) website designed for both foreigners and Koreans.
  • One place for services, secondhand items, borrowing, free items and language exchange.
  • Clear labels for paid vs. free listings, so users know what to expect.
  • Listings are tied to real neighborhoods around campus.
  • User profiles, ratings and reviews build trust between neighbors.

02 · Target users

Who is Neighborly for?

Anyone living near campus who wants to help, share or find something nearby.

🎒

International students

Need furniture when they arrive, help with Korean paperwork, and friends to practise Korean with.

📚

Korean students

Want to sell used textbooks, earn money tutoring, and practise English or other languages.

🏘️

Local residents

Offer services like cleaning or repairs, and give away or lend household items.

🛠️

Skilled foreigners

Offer translation, tutoring, cooking or other skills to people in the neighborhood.

04 · Core features

What users can do

Every feature below is planned to fully work with a real backend and database, not just look good.

1

Register & log in

Create an account with email and a securely stored password.

2

Explore listings

Browse all listings or one category, with newest listings first.

3

Search & filters

Filter by keyword, category, neighborhood, price range and paid/free.

4

Listing details

Photos, description, price, location and the owner's profile and rating.

5

Post a listing

Logged-in users can create, edit, delete and mark listings as done.

6

User profiles

Bio, languages spoken, neighborhood, active listings and reviews.

7

Inquiries

Send a message to a listing owner; owners see inquiries on their profile.

8

Ratings & reviews

Rate other users from 1 to 5 stars with a short comment.

9

English / 한국어

Switch the whole interface between English and Korean with one click.

10

Responsive design

Works comfortably on both desktop and mobile phones.

07 · Launch scope

Starting around Gachon University

Instead of launching across all of Korea, Neighborly starts with the neighborhoods around Gachon University's Global Campus in Seongnam. Neighborhoods are stored as data, so new areas and cities can be added later without changing the code.

Phase 1 · Launch

Bokjeong-dong Taepyeong-dong Sinheung-dong Gachon Univ. Station area

Phase 2 · Later expansion

Other Seongnam neighborhoods Other university areas in Seoul Other cities in Korea

✅ Included in this project

  • All 10 core features listed above
  • Real accounts, listings, reviews and inquiries saved in a database
  • Sample listings so the site looks alive on day one
  • Deployed online with a public link

🚫 Not included (to keep scope realistic)

  • Online payments: users arrange payment in person
  • Real-time chat: replaced by the inquiry system
  • Maps and identity verification
  • Mobile app: the website is mobile-friendly instead
